Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

File Processing

Uploaded files are processed securely and deleted immediately.

ConvertDesk is designed to process files locally in your browser whenever possible. We do not intentionally store uploaded files on a server for the browser-based converter.

Cookies

ConvertDesk does not require account cookies to use the converter. If analytics, advertising, or embedded third-party services are added later, those services may set cookies or similar browser storage according to their own policies.

Third-Party Advertising

We may display third-party ads in the future. Third-party advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, pixels, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, limit repeated ads, detect fraud, and personalize ad content where permitted by law.

Those advertising partners may collect information such as your browser type, device information, IP-derived location, pages visited, referring pages, and ad interactions. We do not grant advertisers access to uploaded file contents.

Third-Party Libraries

The app may load client-side libraries, such as PDF parsing tools, from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard network request information when your browser downloads those scripts.

Data Retention

Generated download files are held only as temporary in-browser object URLs and are revoked after download, reset, page unload, or the next conversion.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@convertdesk.app.
